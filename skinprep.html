Skin prep

Good makeup starts with good skin prep. Preparing your skin helps create a smooth, hydrated base, making your makeup easier to apply and helping it look fresh for longer.

1

Cleanser

Starting with clean skin is important because leftover oil, dirt and skincare can affect how your makeup sits. Cleansing gives you a fresh, smooth base and helps your makeup apply more evenly.

2

Toner

Toner can help refresh and hydrate the skin after cleansing. A well prepped and balanced base can make the products you apply afterwards feel smoother and more comfortable on the skin.

3

Serum

A hydrating serum can give your skin an extra boost of moisture before makeup. This can help prevent foundation and concealer from clinging to dry areas or looking patchy throughout the day.

4

Eye care

The skin around your eyes can easily become dry, which may make concealer look cakey or settle into fine lines. A small amount of eye cream can help keep the area hydrated and create a smoother base.

5

Moisturizer

Moisturizer is an important part of makeup prep because hydrated skin generally creates a smoother base. It can help foundation blend more evenly and prevent your makeup from looking dry or flaky.

6

Sunscreen

Sunscreen helps protect your skin from harmful UV rays and should be the final step of your morning skincare routine. Let it settle into the skin before applying makeup on top.
