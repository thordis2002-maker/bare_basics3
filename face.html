Face

Foundation

Foundation creates a smooth, even base by balancing your skin tone and covering redness or uneven areas.

Concealer

Concealer helps brighten the under-eye area and cover blemishes, redness, or other areas where you want a little extra coverage.

Cream blush

Cream blush adds a natural flush of colour to the cheeks and blends easily into the skin for a fresh, dewy finish.

Bronzer

Bronzer adds warmth and dimension to the face, giving the skin a naturally sun-kissed look.

Powder blush

Powder blush adds colour and definition to the cheeks, giving the face a fresh, healthy-looking finish.

Powder

Powder helps set your makeup in place, reduce shine, and create a smooth, long-lasting finish.
